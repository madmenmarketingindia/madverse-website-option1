About the role

Own the relationship.
Move the work forward.

You’ll be the strategic bridge between ambitious clients and the specialist teams across Madverse.

From understanding the real business challenge to shaping the brief, aligning teams and maintaining momentum, you’ll help turn strong thinking into work that creates measurable impact.

This is a role for someone who combines commercial awareness with empathy, clarity and a genuine respect for creative and specialist craft.

Reports to
Business Director
Works with
Strategy, Creative, Media & Production
Primary focus
Client growth and delivery excellence

What you’ll do

Turn direction
into momentum.

You’ll own the day-to-day relationship while keeping every conversation connected to the bigger business objective.

  1. 01

    Lead client partnerships

    Build trusted senior relationships, understand priorities and become a reliable partner in every important decision.

  2. 02

    Shape stronger briefs

    Translate business challenges into focused briefs that give strategy, creative and specialist teams clear direction.

  3. 03

    Connect the right teams

    Bring together the most relevant capabilities across Madverse and keep everyone aligned around one shared outcome.

  4. 04

    Drive delivery

    Own timelines, priorities, budgets and communication while maintaining the quality and ambition of the work.

  5. 05

    Find growth opportunities

    Spot ways to deepen partnerships, solve emerging challenges and create greater value for the client.

  6. 06

    Raise the standard

    Bring clarity, accountability and considered feedback to every stage of the relationship and delivery process.

What you’ll bring

Experience matters.
Mindset matters more.

We’re looking for someone who can balance commercial thinking, strong relationships and calm, considered delivery.

01

Experience

  • 5–7 years in account management, client servicing or an adjacent strategic role.
  • Experience leading integrated campaigns or multi-disciplinary brand projects.
  • Confidence managing senior stakeholders, budgets, timelines and competing priorities.
  • A strong understanding of strategy, creative development and modern marketing channels.
02

Mindset

  • You listen carefully, ask sharper questions and communicate with clarity.
  • You take ownership without losing sight of collaboration or specialist expertise.
  • You stay composed when priorities shift and turn complexity into a clear next step.
  • You care about the quality of the relationship as much as the quality of the work.
✦

You don’t need to match every line. If the role feels like your next meaningful move, we’d still like to hear from you.

What success looks like

Build trust.
Create momentum.

Your first months are about understanding the context, earning confidence and gradually taking full ownership.

  1. First30days

    Understand the landscape

    Learn the client’s business, meet the specialist teams, understand active work and establish clear communication rhythms.

  2. By60days

    Own the momentum

    Lead day-to-day relationships, improve briefs and workflows, and begin identifying opportunities to create greater value.

  3. By90days

    Shape what comes next

    Become a trusted client partner, confidently lead integrated teams and build a clear growth plan for the relationship.
