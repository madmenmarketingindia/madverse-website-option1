01

Scope of this policy

This policy applies when you visit the Madverse website, contact us, submit a business enquiry, apply for a role, or otherwise share personal information with us through this website.

02

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, company, job information, portfolio, enquiry details and messages.

We may also receive limited technical information such as device type, browser, approximate location, pages visited and referral source through server logs or analytics tools.

03

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess collaboration or employment opportunities, operate and improve the website, understand its performance, maintain security, meet legal obligations and communicate where you have asked us to do so.

04

How we share information

We may share information with relevant Madverse businesses and trusted service providers supporting our website, communications, recruitment or operations. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

05

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies to function correctly, remember preferences, maintain security and understand website usage. You can control non-essential cookies through available consent settings or your browser.

06

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its original purpose and applicable legal, accounting, operational or recruitment requirements.

07

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, update or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent. We may need to verify your identity first.

08

Security and external links

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. External websites follow their own privacy policies.

09

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email team@madmen.in. We may update this policy as our practices or legal obligations change.
