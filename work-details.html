The Challenge

Crax Truffle was a new flavor in a highly competitive snack market. The challenge was to build awareness, create strong differentiation, and drive trial among young, curious audiences.

  • Low awareness for a new flavor variant
  • Standing out in a crowded snack category
  • Driving trial and repeat purchase
  • Creating buzz across digital platforms
